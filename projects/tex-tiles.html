works | product, materials | tex-tiles

One square, endless possibilities.

Welcome to Unstitch. Here, you will find our modular tex-TILES available in our first product range: the Bag Kit. Read on to find out how the TILES are made in our circular X factory.


Unstitch is bred at the intersection of design, mathematics and business. The design philosophy centres around zero-waste and aims to help shape a future where tex-TILES enables ethical consumption.  Why?: the planned obsolescence. 90% of Australian textiles end up in the landfill or get shipped offshore. They continue to pile and we continue to buy. What if we continue to buy but the pile doesn’t grow?

Details: the production
Unstitch's Tiles are made meticulously and precisely using various fabrication methods. We imagine how upcycling can be scaled and this became the 8-stage circular system, which is adaptable to work with different contexts and needs – from community building to corporations.
